Welcome to iLists

Whether you are organizing your weekly grocery run, planning a vacation packing checklist, or managing your daily to-dos, iLists is the ultimate tool to keep your day on track. Stop juggling random notes across different apps and start conquering your day with perfect organization.

Beautifully Simple, Powerfully Flexible

  • Infinite Possibilities: Create any type of list you need. From simple daily checklists to complex project trackers, iLists adapts to your workflow.
  • Effortless Organization: Manage, group, and sort multiple lists in a clean, modern interface designed specifically to get out of your way.
  • Smart Features: Use the built-in "Keep Screen On" toggle while you are grocery shopping or cooking, so you never have to constantly unlock your phone with full hands.
  • Sync & Share: Seamlessly sync your lists across all your devices using iCloud, and share them with family or friends for real-time collaboration.
