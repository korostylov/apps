Welcome to iLists

Whether you are organizing your weekly grocery run, planning a vacation packing checklist, or managing your daily to-dos, iLists is the ultimate tool to keep your day on track. Stop juggling random notes across different apps and start conquering your day with perfect organization.

Beautifully Simple, Powerfully Flexible

  • Infinite Possibilities: Create any type of list you need. From simple daily checklists to complex project trackers, iLists adapts to your workflow.
  • Effortless Organization: Manage, group, and sort multiple lists in a clean, modern interface designed specifically to get out of your way.
  • Smart Features: Use the built-in "Keep Screen On" toggle while you are grocery shopping or cooking, so you never have to constantly unlock your phone with full hands.
  • Sync & Share: Seamlessly sync your lists across all your devices using iCloud, and share them with family or friends for real-time collaboration.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: Jun 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how iLists ("the App") handles your information. Our core philosophy is simple: your data belongs entirely to you. We do not collect, store, sell, or have any access to your personal information or the contents of your lists.

Data Collection and Storage

iLists operates entirely on your device. All data you create within the App—including your checklists, to-dos, and project trackers—is stored directly on your device using Apple's native Core Data framework.

For automatic synchronization across your Apple devices, iLists relies exclusively on your private Apple iCloud account. Your data is synced securely between your devices and Apple's servers. We (the developers) cannot view, access, or retrieve your data.

Third-Party Services and Tracking

We do not use any third-party databases, analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs. Your data never leaves the Apple ecosystem.

Data Sharing and Collaboration

iLists uses native iCloud sharing to securely share your lists with family and friends. This process is handled exclusively through Apple's infrastructure. We have no visibility into the data you share or the users you collaborate with.

Usage and Diagnostic Data

We do not actively track how you use the App. Any diagnostic data (such as anonymous crash reports) is strictly opt-in and managed entirely by Apple through standard App Store analytics. This data contains no personally identifiable information and is used solely to fix bugs and improve the App's stability.

Changes to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or relevant laws. Any updates will be posted directly on this page.
