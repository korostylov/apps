Welcome to iExpire

Stop wasting money on expired food and never miss a crucial document renewal again. Whether you are tracking the milk in your fridge, your passport, daily medications, or family vaccinations, iExpire keeps your life organized and your mind at ease.

Stay Ahead of the Clock

  • Smart Sorting: Items are automatically sorted by expiration date, putting the most urgent items right at the top of your screen.
  • Visual Warnings: Instantly know where you stand with our custom color-coded system: Expired (Red), Expiring Soon (Orange), Approaching (Yellow), and Safe (Green).
  • Push Notifications: Get timely alerts for items that are about to expire so you can take action before it's too late.

Ultimate Organization & Sharing

  • Custom Lists & Filters: Group your items into dedicated lists (e.g., "Fridge," "Medicine Cabinet") and instantly filter to see only what needs your attention.
  • Real-Time Collaboration: Share your lists securely with other iCloud users—perfect for families managing groceries or roommates splitting household supplies.
  • Data Portability: Easily backup, share, or import your entire database using JSON files.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: Jun 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how iExpire ("the App") handles your information. Because you are using the App to track everything from household groceries to sensitive personal documents, we have built iExpire to guarantee your absolute privacy. We do not collect, store, sell, or have any access to your personal inventory.

Data Collection and Storage

iExpire operates entirely on your device. All information you input—including item names, expiration dates, custom timelines, and categories—is stored locally on your device using Apple's native Core Data framework.

For automatic synchronization across your Apple devices, iExpire relies exclusively on your private Apple iCloud account. Your data is synced securely between your devices and Apple's servers. We (the developers) cannot view, access, or retrieve your data.

Third-Party Services and Tracking

We do not use any third-party databases, analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs. Your data never leaves the Apple ecosystem. Additionally, features like JSON data exports are processed entirely locally on your device.

Data Sharing and Collaboration

iExpire uses native iCloud sharing to securely share your inventory lists with roommates, family, or colleagues. This process is handled exclusively through Apple's infrastructure. We have no visibility into the data you share or the users you collaborate with.

Usage and Diagnostic Data

We do not actively track how you use the App. Any diagnostic data (such as anonymous crash reports) is strictly opt-in and managed entirely by Apple through standard App Store analytics. This data contains no personally identifiable information and is used solely to fix bugs and improve the App's stability.

Changes to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or relevant laws. Any updates will be posted directly on this page.
