Welcome to iExpire

Stop wasting money on expired food and never miss a crucial document renewal again. Whether you are tracking the milk in your fridge, your passport, daily medications, or family vaccinations, iExpire keeps your life organized and your mind at ease.

Stay Ahead of the Clock

  • Smart Sorting: Items are automatically sorted by expiration date, putting the most urgent items right at the top of your screen.
  • Visual Warnings: Instantly know where you stand with our custom color-coded system: Expired (Red), Expiring Soon (Orange), Approaching (Yellow), and Safe (Green).
  • Push Notifications: Get timely alerts for items that are about to expire so you can take action before it's too late.

Ultimate Organization & Sharing

  • Custom Lists & Filters: Group your items into dedicated lists (e.g., "Fridge," "Medicine Cabinet") and instantly filter to see only what needs your attention.
  • Real-Time Collaboration: Share your lists securely with other iCloud users—perfect for families managing groceries or roommates splitting household supplies.
  • Data Portability: Easily backup, share, or import your entire database using JSON files.
