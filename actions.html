Welcome to Actions

Stop wondering, "When was the last time I did that?" Actions is your ultimate personal logbook. Whether you are tracking when you last changed the car oil, replaced the water filter, gave your pet their medication, or watered the house plants, Actions gives you a perfect, searchable history of your completed tasks.

Log It and Forget It

  • Instant Recall: Free up your mental space. Log your completed tasks in seconds and let the app remember the history for you.
  • Precision Timing: Record the exact date and time an event occurred, so you always have an accurate timeline.

Perfectly Organized

  • Custom Lists: Keep your history perfectly sorted. Create dedicated lists for your Home, Car, Health, or Pets so your records never get cluttered.
  • Lightning Fast Search: Instantly look up any past action to see exactly when it happened.
  • Clean, Modern Design: Built with a beautifully simple, native iOS interface that gets out of your way and makes logging effortless.
