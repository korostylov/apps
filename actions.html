Welcome to Actions

Stop wondering, "When was the last time I did that?" Actions is your ultimate personal logbook. Whether you are tracking when you last changed the car oil, replaced the water filter, gave your pet their medication, or watered the house plants, Actions gives you a perfect, searchable history of your completed tasks.

Log It and Forget It

  • Instant Recall: Free up your mental space. Log your completed tasks in seconds and let the app remember the history for you.
  • Precision Timing: Record the exact date and time an event occurred, so you always have an accurate timeline.

Perfectly Organized

  • Custom Lists: Keep your history perfectly sorted. Create dedicated lists for your Home, Car, Health, or Pets so your records never get cluttered.
  • Lightning Fast Search: Instantly look up any past action to see exactly when it happened.
  • Clean, Modern Design: Built with a beautifully simple, native iOS interface that gets out of your way and makes logging effortless.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: Jun 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Actions ("the App") handles your information. Our app is designed to be your personal logbook, and we believe your history should remain strictly personal. We do not collect, store, sell, or have any access to the tasks you log.

1. Data Collection and Storage

Actions operates entirely on your device. All data you create within the App—including your events, timestamps, and custom lists—is stored directly on your device using Apple's native Core Data framework.

For automatic synchronization across your Apple devices, Actions relies exclusively on your private Apple iCloud account. Your data is synced securely between your devices and Apple's servers. We (the developers) cannot view, access, or retrieve your data.

2. Third-Party Services and Tracking

We do not use any third-party databases, analytics tools, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs. Your data never leaves the Apple ecosystem.

3. Data Sharing and Collaboration

Actions uses native iCloud sharing to securely share your lists with family and friends. This process is handled exclusively through Apple's infrastructure. We have no visibility into the data you share or the users you collaborate with.

4. Usage and Diagnostic Data

We do not actively track how you use the App. Any diagnostic data (such as anonymous crash reports) is strictly opt-in and managed entirely by Apple through standard App Store analytics. This data contains no personally identifiable information and is used solely to fix bugs and improve the App's stability.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or relevant laws. Any updates will be posted directly on this page.
